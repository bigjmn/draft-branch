## 1. About this policy

DraftBranch is a screenwriting and version-control platform operated by J Nicks Productions (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and disclose it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

It covers the draftbranch.com website, the DraftBranch web editor, the DraftBranch desktop applications for macOS, Windows, and Linux, project invitations and invitation emails, and any temporary or replacement hosting addresses we designate (together, the “Service”).

Words defined in our [Terms of Use](/terms) have the same meaning here. In particular, “Your Content” means scripts, screenplay text, titles, notes, comments, revisions, uploaded files, and other creative material you submit to or create through the Service.

The short version:

- We collect what we need to run your account and store and share your work, plus usage statistics (through Firebase Analytics) that help us understand how the Service is used. We do not run advertising.
- We never sell your personal information, and we never use your scripts to train AI models without your explicit permission.
- Your projects are private unless you invite people to them.
- You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete it, at any time.

## 2. Definitions

**Personal information** (also called personal data) means information about an identified or identifiable living person, such as a name, email address, or account identifier.

**Usage data** means technical information generated automatically when a device connects to the Service, such as an IP address, browser or device type, and the time of a request.

**Data controller.** For the purposes of data-protection laws such as the GDPR, J Nicks Productions is the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

**Service provider** (or processor) means a company that processes personal information on our behalf, under our instructions, to help us operate the Service.

## 3. Information we collect

### Account information

When you create an account with an email address and password, we receive your email address and the display name you provide. Your password is handled by our authentication provider, Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it in hashed form; we never see it.

When you continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo address with us, along with the fact that you signed in with Google. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, or other Google data.

We also keep basic account records: a unique account ID, how you sign in, when the account was created, and whether your email address has been verified.

### Profile information

You can choose to add a profile: a name, job title, company, short bio, and profile picture. Everything except a name is optional. Profile information is visible to people who share projects with you.

### Your Content

To provide the Service, we store the material you create and upload, including:

- Scripts, titles, and screenplay drafts, including drafts saved automatically as you write.
- Revision history: commits, commit messages, branches, merge requests, and merges. Version control is designed to keep earlier revisions (see Section 8).
- Comments, including the passage of the script a comment refers to.
- Files you import, such as Final Draft (FDX) files.

Each revision records who made it and when. The author shown on a revision is your display name, or the first part of your email address if you haven't set a name. Your full email address is not written into revision history.

### Collaboration and invitation information

When a project owner invites someone, we store the invitee’s email address, the role offered (Reader or Editor), who sent the invitation, its status, and its expiry date. We use the address to send the invitation email and to check that the person accepting is the person invited. If you invite someone, please make sure they would expect to hear from you.

We keep a count of invitations sent from each account to prevent abuse.

### Guest use

You can try the editor without an account. To do so we create an anonymous account ID with Firebase Authentication, which holds no name or email address. Guest drafts are saved only in your browser on your device, not on our servers.

### Usage and technical data

Like any online service, the infrastructure we use (described in Section 6) automatically receives technical information when your device connects, such as your IP address, browser and operating-system type, and the date and time of requests. Our providers may record this in operational and security logs, which we use to run, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

**Analytics.** We use Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) to understand how people use the Service so we can improve it. It collects information such as:

- The pages and screens you view and the features you use, such as creating a project, committing, or exporting.
- Device and browser information: device type, operating system, browser, screen size, and language.
- Approximate location (country, region, or city), derived from your IP address.
- When and how often you use the Service, and how you arrived at it (for example, from a search engine or a link).
- A randomly generated identifier that recognizes your browser or app installation across visits.

Analytics never receives the text of your scripts, comments, or other creative content. We don’t send your name or email address to Google Analytics.

We do not use session-recording tools, and we don’t use analytics data for advertising.

### Communications with us

If you use the contact form on our website, we receive your name, email address, subject, and message, which are emailed to us so we can reply. To prevent spam, we also keep a one-way hash of your IP address and the times you sent messages for about an hour; the IP address itself is not stored.

If you email us, or reply to our response, we keep the correspondence and any information you include, so we can respond and keep a record of the request.

### Payment information

The Service is currently free and we do not collect payment information. If we introduce paid features, we will update this policy before collecting any.

## 4. Information stored on your device

**Cookies and browser storage.** DraftBranch does not use advertising cookies. We use cookies and your browser’s local storage for:

- **Essential functions.** Keeping you signed in (Firebase Authentication stores your session in your browser), remembering your light or dark theme preference, and saving guest drafts and local recovery copies of your work.
- **Analytics.** Firebase Analytics sets first-party cookies and similar identifiers (such as `_ga` cookies) to tell visits apart and measure how the Service is used. Where the law requires your consent for these, we will ask for it before setting them.

You can clear this data, or block cookies, in your browser settings. Clearing it signs you out and permanently removes any guest drafts that exist only in that browser. Blocking analytics cookies doesn’t affect your use of the Service. You can also opt out of Google Analytics on every website by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

**Desktop applications.** The desktop app keeps a library on your computer containing projects you create without signing in and offline copies of cloud projects you open, including their revision history. Projects you create without an account stay on your computer and are never sent to us. Changes you make offline to a cloud project are sent to our servers only when you sync. When you sign in with Google in the desktop app, your web browser opens Google’s sign-in page, and the result is passed back to the app directly on your own computer.

Because this data stays on your device, you control it: deleting the library folder or uninstalling the app removes it, and we cannot recover it for you.

## 5. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- Create and manage your account and let you sign in.
- Store, save, sync, version, merge, and export Your Content.
- Make projects available to the collaborators you authorize, show who made each change or comment, and enforce project permissions.
- Send invitation emails on behalf of project owners, and send emails needed to run your account, such as email-address verification.
- Notify you of material changes to the Service, our Terms, or this policy.
- Respond to your requests and provide support.
- Understand how the Service is used, measure which features are useful, find problems, and decide what to improve.
- Protect the Service: prevent abuse and fraud, enforce rate limits, investigate security incidents, and enforce our Terms.
- Comply with legal obligations.

**Legal bases (EEA and UK users).** We process personal information to perform our contract with you (providing the Service you signed up for); for our legitimate interests in securing, maintaining, and improving the Service in ways that don’t override your rights; to comply with legal obligations; and with your consent where we ask for it, including for analytics cookies where consent is required.

**What we don’t do.** We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t use it for advertising or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have turned off Google Analytics’ advertising features (Google signals and ads personalization) and do not link our analytics to any Google advertising product. We don’t send marketing email. We don’t make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

**No model training.** As our Terms of Use state, we will never use your scripts or other creative content to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence or machine-learning models without your prior, explicit permission. DraftBranch does not currently send Your Content to any AI service. If we introduce optional AI-assisted features, we will explain what content they process, and who processes it, before you turn them on.

**Personnel access.** Projects are private, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. We access private content only where reasonably necessary to operate and secure the Service, provide support you ask for, investigate abuse or suspected violations, or comply with the law.

## 6. How we share information

### With people you choose

- **Collaborators.** Members of a project can see its content and history according to their role, along with other members’ names, profile information, comments, and the authorship of revisions. Project owners can see the email addresses they invited.
- **Invitation links.** An invitation link lets anyone who holds it read the project’s committed branches and revision history, without an account, until it expires (after 7 days) or is revoked. That includes anyone the link is forwarded to. Private working drafts are never shown through a link.
- **Exports.** Files you export or download are under your control once they leave the Service.

### With service providers

We use the following providers to operate the Service. They may process personal information only on our behalf and under our instructions, not for their own purposes.

- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud** (Google LLC). Authentication and Google Sign-In, the Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Storage for files and revision history, and Cloud Functions, which run our server-side code and hand invitation emails to Resend. Our database is hosted in the United States. [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy); [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** (Google LLC). Usage statistics, as described in Section 3. Google processes this data to provide analytics to us, and also uses data from sites and apps that use its services to run and improve its own services and to protect against fraud. [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Resend** (Plus Five Five, Inc.) delivers invitation emails and messages sent through our contact form. For invitations, it receives the recipient’s email address and the message contents, including the inviter’s name, the project title, and the invitation link. For contact messages, it receives the name, email address, subject, and message you enter. [Resend Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Vercel** (Vercel Inc.) hosts the draftbranch.com website and web editor and serves the desktop downloads. Like any web host, it receives the technical information described in Section 3 when you visit. [Vercel Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

### For legal reasons

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request; to enforce our Terms; to detect or prevent fraud, security, or technical problems; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or J Nicks Productions. Where the law allows, we will try to notify you before disclosing information about you.

### Business transfers

If operation of DraftBranch transfers through a sale, merger, restructuring, or similar transaction, personal information may be transferred as part of it. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy. Any successor must remain bound by our commitments regarding Your Content, including the restriction on model training.

### Aggregated information

We may share aggregated information that cannot reasonably identify you, such as the total number of accounts. This never includes your creative content.

We are based in the United States, and our database is hosted in the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those where you live.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Account and profile information** is kept while your account exists, and deleted after you ask us to close it, subject to the exceptions below.
- **Your Content** is kept while the project exists. A project owner can delete a project, which removes its drafts, revision history, comments, and invitations from our active systems.
- **Revision history.** Version control is designed to preserve earlier revisions. Deleting text from a current draft does not remove it from earlier commits or branches. Removing it everywhere requires deleting the project.
- **Shared projects.** If you leave a project or close your account, revisions and comments you contributed to someone else’s project may remain in its history, attributed to your name, as part of the shared work.
- **Invitation records** stay with the project so the owner can see who was invited. Invitation links stop working when they expire or are revoked.
- **Analytics data** is kept for 14 months, after which Google Analytics deletes event-level data automatically. Aggregated reports that can’t identify you may be kept longer.
- **Backups and logs.** Copies may persist for a limited period in backups and operational logs kept by our providers before they are overwritten or deleted.
- **Legal holds.** We may keep specific information longer where reasonably necessary to comply with the law, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or enforce our Terms.

Information on your own device (guest drafts and the desktop library) stays there until you delete it.

## 9. Security

Information you send to the Service is encrypted in transit. Information stored by our providers is encrypted at rest. Access to projects is enforced by server-side permission checks, and only project members can read a project’s contents.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. Please use a strong, unique password, keep invitation links private, and contact us right away if you believe your account has been compromised. Don’t send passwords or other sensitive information to us by email.

**Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.** We don’t track you across other companies’ sites or apps, sell your information, or share it for targeted advertising, so these browser signals don’t change how we handle your data. To stop analytics, use the opt-out options in Section 4.
